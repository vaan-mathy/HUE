About the course

The Master’s own words

This course presents quotes taken from the Master’s Basic Level, now the 01-HUE course, for in-depth study and application in daily life.

You learn about self-training meditation and spiritual healing, which helps practitioners understand healing with energy: new to the Western world, but a practice more than 5,000 years old in the East.
