About the program

HUE FACULTY®

Human Universal Energy® (HUE) training program was founded in 1989 by Vietnamese Master Mr. Luong Minh Dang (1942–2007). It teaches the integration of body and soul, operating on the principle that everything exists as energy. At its basic level, the method activates 6 chakras (energy centers) to effectively harness and transfer spiritual energy. This flow restores balance and harmony to the energy systems of both the practitioner and the recipient.

The official curriculum consists of 4 levels (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, and Specialized) spanning 8 seminar units (01 HUE to 08 HUE, formerly Levels 1–20). Upon completing the 01 HUE Basic Level, students can advance through the program, enroll in spiritual HUE Development courses, or access individual coaching.

To ensure authenticity, the program is taught under the registered trademarks “Human Universal Energy®” and “Human Universal Energy (HUE) after Mr. Luong Minh Dang®”. It is delivered exactly as originally taught, exclusively through independent, unaffiliated facilitators who are certified, trained, licensed, and registered with a personal ID number by HUE FACULTY, Inc.

Our founder

About the Founder

Mr. LƯƠNG MINH ÐÁNG (1942 – 2007) was the founder of the Human Universal Energy® (HUE) Teaching. He was born in 1942 in the countryside of Cái Bè, Tiền Giang, Vietnam. In 1972, he conceived the Human Universal Energy® (HUE) method, studied and practiced it up until 1989. On the 20th of July 1989, after 17 years of learning, he founded the HUE School and taught the first Level 3 course to a group of 27 Vietnamese students in California. From that point on, he continued to study, practice, and teach the HUE Method in countries all around the world until he passed away in 2007. In all, he devoted 35 years of his life to teaching the HUE Method to students from 65 different countries.

About the President

Mr. Luong Minh Trung, BSSW, MSWPresident, HUE Faculty

Mr. Trung Luong is the President of the HUE FACULTY® on behalf of MANKIND ENLIGHTENMENT LOVE, Inc. (M.E.L.). He joined the HUE School full-time in December 1995 after earning his degree in Social Work with a concentration in Community.

At a December 1995 seminar in Geneva, Switzerland, founder Master Dang publicly appointed Mr. Trung to lead the H.U.E. Headquarters in Saint Louis, Missouri, and direct all centers worldwide. In this role, he coordinated global seminars, managed the Master’s international travel, and served as the primary liaison for all student and director communications.

After Master Dang’s passing, Mr. Trung has expanded his leadership. Today, he is a certified facilitator for all HUE Levels, a certified M.E.L. Coach providing individual student guidance, and the creator of supplementary courses designed to deepen the understanding of the HUE Philosophy.
