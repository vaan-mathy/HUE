HUE Development Training

Just like Master Dang organized the Boomhoek Retreat and the Tiel Seminar, we continue his legacy with supplemental courses that help HUE students understand the HUE Philosophy more deeply.

They are excerpts of Master Dang’s teaching across the levels, in book form, offered as workshops, retreats, conferences, symposiums and travel experiences. Courses usually last 2 to 3 days.
